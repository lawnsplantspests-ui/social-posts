📋 How to schedule these in Meta Business Suite (free)
  1. Go to business.facebook.com and pick your business.
  2. Open Planner in the left-hand menu.
  3. Click the date you want, then Create post.
  4. Tick both Facebook and Instagram.
  5. Back here, tap 📋 Copy post, then paste it into the post box.
  6. Set the time and click Schedule. Done!

Tip: posting 2× a week (a Monday and a Friday) keeps your pages active without being spammy.
